Dear Ajayi: The Nigerian Film Heading Towards the 2027 Oscars

October 9, 2026 | The Naija Ledger

Dear Ajayi: Nigeria's Hope for the 2027 Oscars Nigerian film Dear Ajayi has been selected to represent Nigeria in the Best International Feature Film category at the 2027 Oscars. Directed by Damilola Orimogunje, the film explores the complicated relationship between two estranged sisters. The selection highlights Nigeria's growing presence in international cinema. However, being submitted does not guarantee an Oscar nomination.
