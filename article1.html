Godwin Obaje Makes Nigerian Football History With 100 NPFL Goals

October 9, 2026 | The Naija Ledger

Nigerian football has another milestone to celebrate as Rangers International striker Godwin Obaje reaches 100 career goals in the Nigeria Premier Football League (NPFL).

A Remarkable Achievement

According to a report by Punch Newspapers, Obaje reached the milestone with a penalty during Rangers International's 1-0 victory over Warri Wolves in Ozoro, Delta State, on September 30, 2026.

The achievement placed him alongside Victor Ezeji and Rabiu Ali as the third player to reach 100 goals in the Nigerian top-flight league.

Hard Work and Persistence

In an interview with Punch Sports Extra, Obaje said he was happy to have achieved the milestone. He explained that his focus throughout his career had been to work hard and score goals.

The striker has represented several Nigerian clubs, including Wikki Tourists, FC Ifeanyi Ubah, Plateau United, Abia Warriors and Rangers International.

A Moment for Nigerian Football

Obaje's achievement highlights the dedication required to remain productive in domestic football over several seasons. It also gives supporters another reason to celebrate the players making their mark in the NPFL.
